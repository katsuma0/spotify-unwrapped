How to get your file from Spotify

One request, two emails, one zip. Five minutes of your time, then some waiting on their end. You only do this once.

  1. 1

    Open your Spotify privacy page

    Sign in with the account you listen on. Any browser works, phone or computer.

    Open the privacy page

    The address is spotify.com/account/privacy. From the Spotify app: tap your profile picture, then Settings and privacy, then Account, then Privacy settings. It opens the same page.

  2. 2

    Scroll to Download your data and tick Extended streaming history

    That one is your whole account, every play since day one, with skips, shuffle, albums and devices. Leave Technical log information off, it is not useful here.

    In a hurry? Account data arrives faster but only covers the last year, with fewer fields. You can tick both.

  3. 3

    Press Request data, then confirm the email

    Spotify emails you right away with a Confirm button. Nothing happens until you press it, so check spam if it has not arrived in a few minutes.

  4. 4

    Wait for the “your data is ready” email

    Account data takes about 5 days. Extended streaming history takes up to 30, usually one to two weeks. The download link in that email stays live for 14 days, so grab it when it lands.

  5. 5

    Download my_spotify_data.zip

    Where it lands: on iPhone, the Files app, in Downloads. On Android, the Files app, in Downloads. On a Mac or PC, your Downloads folder. Do not unzip it.

  6. 6

    Come back here and drop the zip in

    Tap Choose file at the top and pick my_spotify_data.zip, or drag it onto the page. It is read right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
